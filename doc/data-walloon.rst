Walloon-specific Data
=====================

* ``data/walloon/custom_costs_rc.csv`` – custom cost assumptions used by the Walloon configuration. Data provided by ICEDD.
* ``data/custom_powerplants.csv`` – custom power plant modified to include the Walloon (BEWAL) nuclear power plant Tihange as 3 separate units for incremental retirement. Also has a complete list of all nuclear power plants in modeled nodes (copied over from older run's `powerplants_s_adm.csv`).
